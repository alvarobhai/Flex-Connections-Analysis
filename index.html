How to read the model
Firm capacity is the connection capacity that can be served in the hour without relying on unsupported flexibility. The heatmaps show that capacity as a share of the requested connection.

Required flex is the additional MW needed to serve the selected customer load when it exceeds hourly network capacity. The portfolio is dispatched in merit order: cooling → auxiliary → IT workload → solar → storage.

Screening model: this is an analytical screening tool, not a network studies substitute. Constraint profiles are illustrative and intended to support early-stage connection strategy conversations.
